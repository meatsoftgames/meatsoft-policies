Privacy Policy — RodMap

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: RodMap (iPhone and Android) · Developer: MeatSoft · Contact: dev@meatsoft.io

The short version

RodMap is a fishing log that lives on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. We don't run any servers that receive your catches.

What the app stores, and where

RodMap keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Catches you log: the species, weight, length, bait, tackle, notes and the time.
  • Location of each catch, if you allow location: the coordinates, and the name of the water or place. You can deny the location permission; the app still logs catches without a place.
  • Photos you add to a catch, copied into the app's own storage.
  • Sessions and settings, such as your favourite species and units.

Place names

To name a session after the water you fished, RodMap asks your phone's built-in place-name service to look up a catch's coordinates. On iPhone this is Apple's service; on Android it is Google's. They receive the coordinates for that lookup. We don't.

What we never collect

We do not collect or receive your catches, locations, photos, notes or anything you type. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking identifiers.

Export

Export in Settings makes a zip file of your catches and photos. It goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (while using the app): to save where each catch was made. Optional.
  • Camera and Photos: to add a photo to a catch. Optional.

Each permission is requested when you first use the related feature, and each can be changed later in your phone's settings.

Backups and deletion

Your data may be included in your phone's normal backup, according to your own device settings; we have no access to those backups. Deleting a catch in the app removes it from the app's storage. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

RodMap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
