Privacy Policy — Keepsake: Gifts & Dates

Effective date: 13 September 2026 · Developer: MeatSoft · Contact: dev@meatsoft.io

The short version

Keepsake is a private notebook for the people in your life. Everything you put in it stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

Keepsake keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • People you add: their name, any notes you write, and an optional photo.
  • Memories you save: the text, the kind (gift idea, note, milestone), an optional photo, and the time you saved it.
  • Location of a memory, if you leave the location switch on when saving: the coordinates and a place name looked up on your device. You can turn this off for any memory, and you can deny the location permission entirely; the app works without it.
  • Dates such as birthdays and anniversaries, and the reminder schedule for them.

Photos you attach are copied into the app's own storage. Keepsake never writes to your photo library and never uploads photos anywhere. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; they do not go through any server.

What we never collect

We do not collect or receive your name, email, contacts, photos, location, notes, or anything you type. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking identifiers. We have no servers holding your data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (while using the app): to note where you were when you saved a memory. Optional.
  • Camera and Photos: to attach a photo to a memory or a person. Optional.
  • Notifications: to remind you a week before and on the day of a date. Optional.

Each permission is requested only when you first use the related feature, and each can be changed later in iOS Settings.

Backups and deletion

Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) according to your own device settings; we have no access to those backups. Deleting a person, memory, or date in the app removes it from the app's storage. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Keepsake is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
