Privacy Policy — Orbit: Let Go

Effective date: 2 July 2026 · Developer: MeatSoft · Contact: dev@meatsoft.io

The short version

Orbit is a game. We don't want your data — we show ads to keep the game free, and we collect anonymous gameplay statistics to make the game better. We never sell personal data, we don't use cross-app tracking identifiers (no IDFA), and there are no accounts, emails, or profiles.

What is collected, and by whom

Advertising — Google AdMob. Orbit shows an ad on the game-over screen and an optional rewarded ad (the revive). Google's SDK collects device information (device model, OS version, IP-derived coarse location, app identifier, and device identifiers used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention) to serve and measure ads. In the UK/EEA, ads are only requested according to your consent choice (see below). Orbit does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or App Tracking Transparency — ads are not based on tracking you across other apps. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Gameplay analytics — Google Firebase Analytics. We collect anonymous gameplay events (e.g. a run started or ended, the score reached, whether a revive was used, settings toggles) tied to an app-scoped identifier that cannot be used to identify you or follow you across apps. Advertising-identifier collection is disabled. We use this solely to understand difficulty, retention, and stability of the game. See Firebase privacy.

Leaderboards — Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, scores you submit are handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Orbit only sends your score. You can play without Game Center.

What we never collect: name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, health data, or anything typed. There are no accounts and no chat.

Your choices

  • UK/EEA consent: on first launch you'll see a consent message (Google's certified consent tool). You can consent, or manage options to decline — declining means no ads are served to you at all. You can change your choice any time via Settings → Privacy in the game.
  • Sound, haptics and other preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Deleting data: gameplay analytics are anonymous and cannot be tied back to you; uninstalling the app stops all collection. For any request, contact dev@meatsoft.io.

Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; the ad configuration requests non-child-directed treatment consistent with the app's store rating.

Data retention

We retain aggregated analytics to analyze the game over time. Google retains ad-serving data per its own policies. We hold no personal data ourselves.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
